# LinkedIn Voice & Editorial Standard (Claude + Apple)

The official writing standard for Aditya Chouhan's LinkedIn content. Built at the intersection of **Apple's editorial minimalism** (high contrast, spacious typography, zero fluff, clean structure) and **Anthropic/Claude's intellectual posture** (calm authority, practitioner depth, verified proof, counter-intuitive insight).

## 1. Core Posture & Tone

- **Calm, Unshakable Authority**: Speak as a builder who has tested the systems in production. No false excitement, no cheerleading.
- **Practitioner Depth**: Ground every claim in concrete mechanics, headcount thresholds, API workflows, or measurable baselines.
- **Understated Confidence**: Let the architecture and receipts do the convincing. Never boast or use synthetic superlatives.

## 2. Forbidden Anti-Patterns (Zero Tolerance)

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Is Banned | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| *"I'm thrilled to announce..."* | Low-status corporate boilerplate | *"We built X to solve Y. Here is the architecture."* |
| *"Agree? Thoughts?"* | Desperate engagement baiting | High-conviction conclusion or actionable CTA |
| Emojis as bullet points (🚀, 🔥, 💡) | Cluttered, amateur aesthetic | Clean numbers (`1.`, `2.`) or subtle Unicode (`•`, `→`) |
| URLs inside the post body | Suppresses organic reach by 40–60% | Link in pinned first comment + comment keyword trigger |
| Fabricated vulnerability / melodrama | Manipulative, low-trust | Objective diagnosis of technical/pipeline friction |

## 3. The 6-Part Anatomy of a 10/10 Post

1. **The Hard Hook (Line 1–2)**:
   - Must state a sharp, contrarian truth in under 12 words.
   - Example: *"Most LinkedIn content produces vanity impressions, not pipeline."*
2. **The Diagnosis (Line 3–5)**:
   - 2–3 sentences defining the exact friction the reader experiences.
3. **The Architectural Shift (Line 6–8)**:
   - The thesis or engineered framework that resolves the friction.
4. **The Technical Pillars (3 to 5 Points)**:
   - Each point starts with a bold headline, followed by a crisp 1–2 sentence operational breakdown with specific numbers or mechanisms.
5. **The Open-Source Asset / Deliverable**:
   - Clear statement of what is available (MIT repo, Python CLI, Excalidraw maps, schemas).
6. **The Velocity CTA & Pinned Comment**:
   - Comment keyword trigger (e.g., *"Comment 'FUNNEL' below..."*).
   - Followed by 3–5 semantic hashtags at the bottom (`#GTM #B2BMarketing #ClaudeAI #AIAutomation #OpenSource`).
   - Accompanied by the exact copy for the pinned first comment.

## 4. Visual Pairing Guidelines

Always pair with high-DPI Apple + Claude visual assets:
- **Canvas**: Warm ivory/cream `#FAF7F2`
- **Cards**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` with 16–20px rounded corners and 1px border `#E8E2DA`
- **Text**: Dark charcoal ink `#1F1E1D`
- **Primary Accent**: Signature Terracotta `#D97757`
- **Brand Colors**: Authentic LinkedIn Blue `#0A66C2`, YouTube Red `#FF0000`
- **Author**: Clean circular avatar with white border and drop shadow
