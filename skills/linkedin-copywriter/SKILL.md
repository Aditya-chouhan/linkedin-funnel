# Write a LinkedIn Post (Claude + Apple Standard)

Drafts or edits high-converting LinkedIn posts adhering to the signature **Claude + Apple editorial style**: calm authority, practitioner depth, minimal fluff, contrarian hooks, technical pillars, velocity comment CTAs, and pinned first comment delivery.

## 1. Preparation & Voice Alignment

1. Read `context/voice/linkedin-voice.md` and `context/voice/formats.md`.
2. Understand the author: **Aditya Chouhan**, GTM Engineer & AI Builder.
3. Identify the central claim, factual proof, target audience, and the asset to pair.
4. Pick the appropriate format from `context/voice/formats.md`:
   - *Architecture Teardown*
   - *Content Waterfall Breakdown*
   - *Proof / Benchmark Post*
   - *Open-Source Release*

## 2. Copy Execution Rules

- **The Hook**: A sharp, counter-intuitive truth stated in under 12 words. No greetings ("Hey connections"), no fake hype ("Excited to share!").
- **The Body**: Structured technical teardown. 3 to 5 distinct points with bold concept titles and 1–2 sentences explaining the *exact operational mechanism*.
- **No External Links in Body**: Never place raw URLs in the post body. Keep the post copy 100% native to preserve algorithmic distribution.
- **The Velocity CTA**: Include a single-keyword comment trigger (e.g., *"Comment 'FUNNEL' below..."*).
- **Semantic Hashtags**: Exactly 3 to 5 relevant tags at the bottom (`#GTM #B2BMarketing #ClaudeAI #AIAutomation #OpenSource`).
- **The Pinned First Comment**: Always generate the exact text for the pinned first comment containing the destination URL.
